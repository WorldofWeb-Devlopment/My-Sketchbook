About the Artist

My Story

I'm a digital illustrator who believes that not every line needs to be straight. I've always loved the feeling of a rough pencil sketch over polished 3D graphics. This portfolio is an extension of that love—a digital space that feels like flipping through a physical notebook.

Creative Journey

2018

Bought my first sketchbook

Started doodling in the margins of my math homework.

2021

Discovered Digital Art

Got a drawing tablet and started realizing the endless possibilities of undo (ctrl+z is a lifesaver).

2026

Built this Portfolio

Finally brought my sketchbook to the web!
