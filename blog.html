The Magic of Watercolors

March 16, 2026 • 5 min read

Welcome to another page in my sketchbook! Today, we're diving into the unpredictable, beautifully chaotic world of watercolors. Unlike pencils or markers, watercolor has a mind of its own. It flows where the water takes it.

Embracing the Mistakes

One of the first things you learn is that you can't control everything. Sometimes the colors bleed together perfectly, and sometimes they create a muddy mess. But that's exactly where the magic happens.

"Let the water do the work, you are just there to guide it."

I usually start out by dropping clean water onto my cold-pressed paper, just creating random shapes. Then, I tap a loaded brush of Ultramarine Blue and watch it crawl across the wet surface. It's mesmerizing.

Thanks for reading! Keep your brushes wet and your imagination dry. Wait, no, the other way around.
